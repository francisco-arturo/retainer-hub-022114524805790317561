## The one sentence to say about it

> "This is the whole HoldThatSmile loop: a customer orders a kit, follows production in real time, and the lab team runs the same order from payment to delivery without a spreadsheet."

## What is deliberately fake

- All customers, orders, payments and tracking numbers are fictional seed data in `js/data.js`.
- The customer is signed in automatically for the demo; there is no password flow.
- The staff PIN (`2468`) is checked in the browser. Production uses server-verified staff sessions and roles.
- Checkout is simulated: test card details are validated for shape, then discarded. No charge is made and no card data is stored.
- Email, SMS and carrier notifications are represented in the in-app update feed, not sent.
- State is saved to `localStorage` so changes survive a refresh. The footer's **Reset demo data** restores the seed.
- The app is a static front end; it is not connected to a database, payment provider or manufacturing system.

## What is implemented for real

- End-to-end order creation with validation, pricing, order IDs, delivery estimate and payment summary.
- A seven-stage manufacturing model with stage-dependent lab, kit, shipping and payment states.
- Admin stage advancement, payment collection, filters, search, production board, customer book and payment ledger.
- Customer notifications generated by staff actions.
- Account and notification preference updates.
- Responsive layouts for the dashboard, checkout, order tracking and operations console.

## If something fails while they look

- Return to http://127.0.0.1:4176/#/overview.
- Use **Reset demo data** in the footer to restore the seeded state.
- Re-run `npm start` if the local server stopped; the app has no build step.
- Re-run verification with `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\init.ps1`.

## Not built (the production next step)

1. Server-side authentication, role-based staff access and an audit log.
2. A real database and API (orders, customers, manufacturing stages, payments).
3. Stripe or equivalent payment intents, refunds and webhook reconciliation.
4. Transactional email/SMS and carrier tracking webhooks.
5. Lab integrations for impression intake, CAD/CAM files and shipping labels.
